# 🪨 Pet Rock Simulator

> *A pet that does almost nothing. Keep an eye on it anyway.*

**[▶ Adopt your rock now](https://sarthak213.github.io/pet-rock-simulator/)**. It isn't going anywhere. That's kind of the point.

---

## What is this?

After years of research, we've built the most realistic pet rock simulation ever made. Our engine faithfully reproduces every behaviour of a real pet rock, including:

- Sitting there
- Continuing to sit there
- Sitting there with feelings (new in v1)

## Gameplay

1. Open the page.
2. Look at the rock.
3. Optionally: pet it, talk to it, take it for a walk (it stays home), show it a pebble, or do nothing.
4. Do nothing five times in a row. Unlock enlightenment. We're not kidding.

## System requirements

- **Minimum:** Any browser from the last decade.
- **Recommended:** Patience.
- **Rock:** Not included. A digital rock is included instead. Please don't ask for a physical rock.

## Installation

You don't install it. It's a web page. If you insist:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/sarthak213/pet-rock-simulator.git
```

Then open `index.html`. There's no build step, no framework, and no `node_modules`. The rock wouldn't stand for it.

## FAQ

**Is my rock alive?**
Legally? No.

**My rock hasn't moved in three days. Should I be worried?**
Your rock is in excellent health.

**Can my rock die?**
No. It's outlived dinosaurs, and it'll outlive your browser tab.

**Where is my save data?**
In your browser's `localStorage`. Clearing your site data releases your rock back into the wild. The wild looks exactly like the wild where you got it.

**I petted my rock 400 times. Is that too many?**
The rock has noted every one.
